## Canonical demo metaphor

- Fighters: Lakebase versus the selected competitor.
- Ringside: the selected customer personas.
- Fight card: persona-relevant proof rounds.
- Referee: identical task, data, and verifier.
- Scoreboard: measured evidence.

The setup prompt is **“Who’s at ringside?”**, never “Choose your fighter.” The ringside portraits are intentionally lovable and dorky, with one literal role cue per character. They do not appear in the timed proof lanes.

Palette:

- Lakebase red: `#FF3621`
- Ring black: `#101214`
- Canvas cream: `#F7F3EA`
- Neutral steel: `#767B80`

All three are intentionally flat, high-contrast, and readable at presentation size. The artwork is an original homage to late-1980s console sports games as a genre; it contains no third-party characters, sprites, wordmarks, or other borrowed visual assets, and it is not affiliated with or derived from any specific game or franchise.
